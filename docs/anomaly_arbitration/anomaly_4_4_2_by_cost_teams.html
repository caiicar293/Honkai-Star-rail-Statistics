Import Your Roster

Reliquary Archiver is a free, open-source tool that reads your account data directly from the game. It captures your characters and eidolons — enough for this page to filter down to teams you can actually build. Only the characters section of the export is read; relics, light cones, materials, and gacha history are ignored and never leave your browser.
1
Download reliquary-archiver-pktmon-x64.exe from the Reliquary Archiver releases page.
(the pcap version requires extra software — pktmon works out of the box on Windows 10+)
2
Run the .exe and wait until it says "Waiting for login…"
Windows or your antivirus may flag it — this is a false positive. Click "More info" → "Run anyway" or add an exception.
3
Open Honkai: Star Rail, log in, and enter the game — the archiver captures your data automatically.
4
Find the archive_output-*.json file it creates, then upload it below.
